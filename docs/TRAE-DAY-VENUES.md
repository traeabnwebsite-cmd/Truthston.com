# Trae Day — verified venue collection

Researched October 27, 2026. Twelve physical venues are represented, not separate Houston/Katy city editions. Eight are locations of the main family celebration; four are clearly identified weekend concert, comedy, film or dodgeball venues. This is a sourced subset, not an assertion that every event from every year has been reconciled.

Each card links its evidence and lists only the individually documented years. Matching green worn-ink circles establish the Trae Day family. Historical location does not establish a visitor’s attendance or award eligibility; these are unissued, zero-credit preview designs. Future campaigns require organizer-defined verification. No existing local activity awards or real account balances were changed.

## Naming and historical reconciliation

- **TSU, 2009:** the firsthand report identifies the stadium parking lot. Do not change this to inside a stadium or another campus venue.
- **Reliant / NRG, 2013–2014:** use campus parking grounds, not a claim that the main festival occupied the football field. The older Reliant name and newer NRG name refer to the same campus; they are one collectible.
- **Aveva / SaberCats:** one physical stadium at 12131 Kirby Drive. Supported editions here are 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2025. A gap in this list does not establish that an event did not happen.
- **2012:** a June advance report and a 2015 retrospective name Northwest Mall. Post-event year-end reporting instead identifies a community fair at Emancipation Park; a Chronicle photograph explicitly dates that park event July 22, 2012. The collection follows those post-event records and does not issue a Northwest Mall stamp for 2012.
- **2010 unresolved:** AllHipHop’s June 22, 2010 announcement and the 2015 Houston Press retrospective name the former International Ballroom, while a June 2012 Houston Press retrospective names Northwest Mall for 2010. An announcement alone does not settle where the event ultimately occurred. Both candidates remain outside this verified collection pending a dated event photograph, held-event report or organizer archive.
- **2026 unresolved naming:** a vault/AllHipHop record calls the venue “Silver Katy Stadium.” This has not been verified as an actual venue name and must not become a Katy city stamp. The Defender’s 2026 community-impact interview does not supply enough precise venue evidence to resolve the name. The record remains a research lead, not published venue proof.
- **No inferred years:** 2019, 2020, 2024 and other undocumented venue/year combinations are not silently filled from neighboring editions. The collection is expandable when evidence arrives.
- **Weekend distinction:** White Oak Music Hall, House of Blues, UH and MATCH are weekend program venues, not substitutes for the main Family Fun Day location. UH’s 2023 schedule misspells Holman as “Helman”; the 2021 schedule uses Holman. The UI retains the documented facility and explains this in its source note.
