# WABN Radio and Studio ABN — reference-led rebuild

October 28, 2026. Original ABN/WABN environments, informed by the following references.

## References inspected

- [The Breakfast Club official site](https://thebreakfastclub.iheart.com/) and [its Trae interview page](https://thebreakfastclub.iheart.com/content/2017-08-16-trae-tha-truth-on-giving-back-to-the-community-why-hes-banned-from-radio/). The interview-room photograph reviewed during research: https://image.okayplayer.com/1411380.jpg?format=jpg&width=960 . Shared dark interview desk, articulated microphone arms, individual mic flags, playout screens, guest seating and a media backdrop informed WABN's arrangement.
- [Hot 107.9 / Hot Spot ATL official Durtty Boyz photo gallery](https://hotspotatl.com/playlist/the-durtty-boyz-show-live-on-hot-107-9-2018-exclusive-photos/) and [J-Nicks archive](https://hotspotatl.com/tag/j-nicks/). Reviewed official photographs for fabric wall finishes, branded interview backdrops and the artist-interview context. WABN retains its own identity and is not presented as a replica of a specific current Hot 107.9 room.
- [Patchwerk Studio 9000 official room/equipment description](https://www.patchwerk.com/studios/the-studios/studio-9000), its official control-room photograph (https://patchwerk.com/images/assets/studios/studio9000/9000%2002.png), and [the studio architect's project description](https://rbdg.com/project/patchwerk-recordings/). Large-format SSL workflow, prominent main monitors, nearfields, outboard racks, warm wood finishes, acoustic treatment and sightlines between control room and isolated recording areas informed Studio ABN.

## Implemented translation

WABN: rounded five-position interview desk, five rolling chairs with actual floor contact, five articulated microphones with WABN flags and cables, a small broadcast console, mounted playout displays, camera, wall media screen, modest ON AIR indicator, shelves and acoustic slats. The screen and table open the existing playlist page.

Studio ABN: large-format console scaled to approximately 6.9 world units across its outer cabinet, channel controls and meter bridge, session display, large main monitors and nearfields, outboard racks, engineer chair, separate glazed vocal booth with mic/pop filter/rug, acoustic treatment and a rear-side lounge. Knobs and faders use instancing rather than one draw call per control. The console opens the existing Studio page; recording, booking and live production are not implemented.

Both rooms: dedicated ceiling lights, ceiling acoustic clouds, reserved circulation space, human-scale seat/table heights, layer 2 isolation, closer room cameras and no floating page companion above the desks. Window reflections are restrained geometry rather than a blur effect. All geometry and panel textures are generated in code; reference photographs are not shipped as site assets.

Ground-floor checkout, side shelves, doors and elevator placement are outside this redesign. Existing left-side elevator opening and landing are preserved.
